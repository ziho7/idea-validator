# Decision report template

Adapt length to mode and answer in the user's language. This is an outline, not a word quota.

## Decision

**Action:** test a small experiment / narrow or change / defer / insufficient evidence.
**Confidence:** low / medium / high with a concrete reason.

State the original idea, the decisive finding and the crucial unknown. Cite evidence
IDs and original links. Never announce “validated” without naming the hypothesis.

## Scope and hypotheses

Research date, mode, audience/market/language, time window, supplied facts, assumptions,
executed query/page counts and actual stop reason.

| ID | Hypothesis | Status | Evidence | Unknowns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Who hurts, when, and why

Strongest pain clusters, triggering situations, reported recurrence, consequences
and workarounds. Keep inferred severity separate from reported costs; no market extrapolation.

## Evidence ledger

| ID / original link | Date / access | Segment and context | Short excerpt or paraphrase | Behavior / payment | Hypothesis / stance | Limits / group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Quote only inspected material and label translations. Include material counterevidence.
Link evidence.json if produced. Distinguish inspected pages, experience groups, vendor
sources, promotion and unverified leads; do not report source count as customer count.
For supplied-only material, label its provenance and verification status, leave missing
URLs/dates unknown, and make conclusions conditional. Mark synthetic scenarios as such.

## Alternatives and counterevidence

| Alternative | Already solves | Remaining gap: observed or inferred | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use current original docs for functionality. Absence from inspected docs is not proof
a feature does not exist. Include satisfied users, resolved issues, switching friction
and simpler process alternatives when found.

## Decision dimensions

Discuss problem reality, severity, alternative gap, solution fit, payment/adoption
and evidence confidence independently. Explain conflicts and segment changes. Avoid
composite market scores or success probabilities.

## Cheapest useful next experiment

- Unresolved hypothesis and why it changes the decision.
- Participant screening criteria, without invented contacts.
- A real task, concierge service, prototype or purchase test.
- Observable behavior: use, repeat use, time saved, switching or actual payment.
- Proposed sample/threshold/timebox, rationale and limitations.
- Next decision if it succeeds or fails.

Praise and hypothetical willingness to pay are not purchases. Experiments remain
proposals unless the user authorizes execution.

## Coverage and limits

Executed queries, source families, unavailable pages, uncertain/old dates, selection
bias, unknown buyer identity and unresolved hypotheses. “Not found” is not “nonexistent”.
